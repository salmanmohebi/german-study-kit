---
title: Präpositionen mit Dativ
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-04-praep-dativ
tags: [a2, praepositionen, dativ]
---

- Ich komme gerade {{c1::aus}} der Bibliothek. | I've just come from the library. | *aus* = out of an enclosed space, always **dative**.
- Wir fahren {{c1::mit}} dem Fahrrad zur Arbeit. | We cycle to work. | *mit* + dative expresses means of transport.
- {{c1::Nach}} dem Kurs trinken wir noch einen Kaffee. | After the class we'll have a coffee. | *nach* + dative for time; also for countries and cities without an article.
- Sie übernachtet {{c1::bei}} ihren Großeltern. | She's staying overnight at her grandparents'. | *bei* + dative = at someone's place; note *Eltern* → *seinen Eltern*.
- Das Geschenk ist {{c1::von}} meiner Tante. | The present is from my aunt. | *von* + dative marks origin, sender or possession.
- {{c1::Seit}} zwei Wochen habe ich Urlaub. | I've been on holiday for two weeks. | *seit* + dative + **present tense** for something still ongoing.
- Wir gehen morgen {{c1::zum}} Arzt. | We're going to the doctor tomorrow. | *zum* = *zu dem*, a compulsory contraction.
- {{c1::Gegenüber}} dem Bahnhof gibt es ein Café. | Opposite the station there's a café. | *gegenüber* + dative; it may also stand after the noun.
- Ich arbeite {{c1::seit}} 2023 in dieser Firma. | I've worked in this company since 2023. | *seit* + a point in time works the same as *seit* + a duration.
- Fahren Sie {{c1::zur}} nächsten Kreuzung. | Drive to the next junction. | *zur* = *zu der*; the adjective takes the dative ending *-en*.
- {{c1::Außer}} mir war niemand da. | Apart from me nobody was there. | *außer* + dative, often followed by a pronoun.
- Sie kommt {{c1::aus}} der Schweiz. | She comes from Switzerland. | Countries **with** an article use *aus der/dem*; others just *aus*.
- {{c1::Beim}} Kochen höre ich Podcasts. | While cooking I listen to podcasts. | *beim* + a capitalized infinitive = "while doing" something.
- Wir treffen uns {{c1::nach}} der Arbeit. | We're meeting after work. | *nach der Arbeit* — feminine dative *der*, easy to confuse with genitive.
