---
title: Wechselpräpositionen (Vertiefung)
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-06-wechsel-vertiefung

---

- Ich setze mich {{c1::auf}} das Sofa. | I sit down on the sofa. | *sich setzen* is directional → accusative; *sitzen* would take the dative.
- Sie sitzt schon {{c1::auf}} dem Sofa. | She's already sitting on the sofa. | Static position → dative *dem Sofa*.
- Häng die Jacke {{c1::in}} den Schrank. | Hang the jacket in the wardrobe. | *hängen* as a transitive verb = directional → accusative.
- Die Jacke hängt {{c1::im}} Schrank. | The jacket is hanging in the wardrobe. | Intransitive *hängen* = position → dative; *im* = *in dem*.
- Wir steigen {{c1::in}} den Zug ein. | We're getting on the train. | *einsteigen in* + accusative — you move into the train.
- Das Foto steht {{c1::auf}} dem Regal. | The photo is standing on the shelf. | *stehen* + dative; *stellen* + accusative is its directional twin.
- Er legt das Handy {{c1::neben}} den Teller. | He puts the phone next to the plate. | *legen* + accusative; *liegen* + dative.
- Das Handy liegt {{c1::neben}} dem Teller. | The phone is lying next to the plate. | Same preposition, different case — the verb decides.
- Ich gehe heute {{c1::ins}} Kino. | I'm going to the cinema today. | *ins* = *in das*; destinations with *in* take the accusative.
- Wir sitzen schon {{c1::im}} Kino. | We're already sitting in the cinema. | Being somewhere → dative *im Kino*.
- Stell die Blumen {{c1::vor}} das Fenster. | Put the flowers in front of the window. | *stellen* = directional, so accusative neuter *das*.
- Die Kinder spielen {{c1::hinter}} dem Haus. | The children are playing behind the house. | *spielen* has no goal here → dative.
- Sie fährt jeden Sommer {{c1::an}} die Nordsee. | She goes to the North Sea every summer. | Travelling to a coast → *an die*; being there → *an der*.
- Der Hund springt {{c1::auf}} den Stuhl. | The dog jumps onto the chair. | *springen auf* + accusative because the position changes.
