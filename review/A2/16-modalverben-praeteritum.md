---
title: Modalverben im Präteritum
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-16-modal-praeteritum
tags: [a2, modalverben, praeteritum]
---

- Ich {{c1::konnte}} dich nicht erreichen. | I couldn't reach you. | Modals lose the umlaut and add *-te*: *können → konnte*.
- Wir {{c1::mussten}} eine Stunde warten. | We had to wait for an hour. | *müssen → musste*; the Perfekt *hat warten müssen* is rare in speech.
- Er {{c1::wollte}} eigentlich mitkommen. | He actually wanted to come along. | *eigentlich* signals a plan that didn't happen.
- Sie {{c1::durfte}} als Kind nicht fernsehen. | As a child she wasn't allowed to watch TV. | *dürfen → durfte*; *als Kind* + no article.
- {{c1::Konntest}} du gut schlafen? | Were you able to sleep well? | *du* form: **konntest**, with *-te-* before the ending.
- Ich {{c1::sollte}} eigentlich lernen. | I was supposed to be studying. | *sollen → sollte* also serves as its own Konjunktiv II form.
- Wir {{c1::mochten}} den Film nicht. | We didn't like the film. | *mögen → mochte*, with *ch* replacing *g*.
- Sie {{c1::wollten}} nur helfen. | They only wanted to help. | Plural *-en*: *wollten*. Position of *nur* narrows the verb phrase.
- Er {{c1::musste}} den Termin verschieben. | He had to postpone the appointment. | The infinitive still closes the Satzklammer in the past.
- Ihr {{c1::durftet}} nicht rein. | You all weren't allowed in. | *ihr* form ends in **-tet**: *durftet, konntet, musstet*.
- Ich {{c1::konnte}} gestern nicht kommen, weil ich krank war. | I couldn't come yesterday because I was ill. | Even in a subordinate clause the modal is last: *krank war*.
- Als Studentin {{c1::musste}} sie viel arbeiten. | As a student she had to work a lot. | *als* + feminine noun for a past role.
- Wir {{c1::konnten}} das Problem endlich lösen. | We were finally able to solve the problem. | *endlich* sits in the middle field, before the object.
- {{c1::Wollte}} er nicht schon längst da sein? | Wasn't he supposed to be here long ago? | Past modal + *sein* as the final infinitive for an unmet expectation.
