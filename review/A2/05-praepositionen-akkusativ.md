---
title: Präpositionen mit Akkusativ
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-05-praep-akkusativ
tags: [a2, praepositionen, akkusativ]
---

- Das Geschenk ist {{c1::für}} meinen Neffen. | The present is for my nephew. | *für* + accusative; *Neffe* is an n-noun → *Neffen*.
- Der Weg führt {{c1::durch}} den Wald. | The path leads through the forest. | *durch* + accusative for movement through a space.
- Er hat nichts {{c1::gegen}} dich. | He has nothing against you. | *gegen* + accusative, literally and figuratively.
- {{c1::Ohne}} deine Hilfe schaffe ich das nicht. | Without your help I won't manage it. | *ohne* + accusative; often used without an article: *ohne Auto*.
- Wir sind einmal {{c1::um}} den Block gelaufen. | We walked once around the block. | *um* + accusative for going around something and for clock times.
- Der Brief ist {{c1::für}} Sie. | The letter is for you. | Formal *Sie* is unchanged in the accusative.
- Wir fahren {{c1::durch}} die Stadt. | We're driving through the town. | Feminine accusative *die* is unchanged — the case shows on masculines.
- {{c1::Gegen}} acht Uhr sind wir da. | We'll be there around eight. | *gegen* + a time means "approximately".
- Sie kämpft {{c1::für}} ihre Rechte. | She fights for her rights. | Plural accusative *ihre Rechte*; *für* never takes the dative.
- Ich mache das {{c1::ohne}} jeden Zweifel. | I'm doing that without any doubt. | *jeden* is the masculine accusative of *jeder*.
- {{c1::Um}} den See herum führt ein Weg. | A path leads around the lake. | *um ... herum* emphasizes a complete circle.
- Er hat sich {{c1::gegen}} das Angebot entschieden. | He decided against the offer. | *sich entscheiden für/gegen* + accusative.
- Der Zug fährt {{c1::durch}} einen Tunnel. | The train goes through a tunnel. | Masculine *ein* → **einen** after *durch*.
- Wir bleiben {{c1::bis}} nächsten Freitag. | We're staying until next Friday. | *bis* + accusative; with an article it usually pairs up: *bis zum Freitag*.
