title: Personalpronomen Akkusativ / Dativ
level: A2

- Ich schenke {{c1::es}} ihr zum Geburtstag. | I'm giving it to her for her birthday. | Two pronouns: **accusative first**, dative second — the reverse of noun order.
- Er gibt {{c1::ihm}} den Schlüssel. | He gives him the key. | Pronoun + noun: the **pronoun** comes first, whatever its case.
- Sie hat {{c1::mich}} nicht gesehen. | She didn't see me. | *sehen* + accusative → *mich*.
- Das Paket? Ich schicke {{c1::es}} dir morgen. | The parcel? I'll send it to you tomorrow. | Accusative *es* precedes dative *dir*.
- Kannst du {{c1::uns}} den Weg zeigen? | Can you show us the way? | *zeigen* takes dative person + accusative thing.
- Ich habe {{c1::ihn}} gestern angerufen. | I called him yesterday. | *anrufen* + accusative — English "call to" misleads here.
- Gib {{c1::mir}} bitte deine Adresse. | Please give me your address. | Dative pronoun immediately after the imperative verb.
- Wir treffen {{c1::euch}} um acht am Bahnhof. | We're meeting you at eight at the station. | **euch** covers both accusative and dative for *ihr*.
- Ich glaube {{c1::dir}}. | I believe you. | *glauben* + dative for a person: *dir*, not *dich*.
- Das Buch gefällt {{c1::ihnen}} sehr. | They like the book a lot. | Lowercase *ihnen* = to them; capitalized *Ihnen* = to you (formal).
- Hast du {{c1::sie}} schon gefragt? | Have you asked her yet? | Feminine *sie* is identical in nominative and accusative.
- Ich bringe {{c1::es}} euch nachher. | I'll bring it to you later. | Accusative *es* first, then dative *euch*, then the time adverb.
- Sie erklärt {{c1::es}} ihm noch einmal. | She explains it to him once more. | The pair **es ihm** is a fixed order you can memorize as a chunk.
- Ich kann {{c1::dich}} gut verstehen. | I can understand you well. | *verstehen* + accusative; the infinitive closes the Satzklammer.
