---
title: Indirekte Fragen
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-14-indirekte-fragen
tags: [a2, nebensaetze, fragen]
---

- Weißt du, wo der Bahnhof {{c1::ist}}? | Do you know where the station is? | An indirect question is a subordinate clause → verb **last**.
- Ich weiß nicht, {{c1::ob}} sich das lohnt. | I don't know whether that's worth it. | Yes/no questions become **ob**-clauses; there is no *if* here.
- Können Sie mir sagen, wie viel das {{c1::kostet}}? | Can you tell me how much that costs? | Politer than the direct question — a very useful A2 formula.
- Er fragt, wann der Film {{c1::anfängt}}. | He's asking when the film starts. | A separable verb stays **whole** at the end: *anfängt*.
- Ich möchte wissen, warum du nicht {{c1::angerufen}} hast. | I'd like to know why you didn't call. | Perfekt in a subclause: participle then auxiliary.
- Sie hat gefragt, {{c1::ob}} wir Hilfe brauchen. | She asked whether we need help. | *ob* never takes *dass* alongside it.
- Wissen Sie, wem dieser Koffer {{c1::gehört}}? | Do you know whose suitcase this is? | *wem* is the dative of *wer*, because *gehören* takes the dative.
- Ich habe keine Ahnung, was das {{c1::bedeutet}}. | I have no idea what that means. | *keine Ahnung haben* + indirect question is very idiomatic.
- Sag mir bitte, wen du {{c1::eingeladen}} hast. | Please tell me whom you invited. | *wen* is the accusative of *wer* — English often drops the distinction.
- Er will wissen, wie lange wir {{c1::bleiben}}. | He wants to know how long we're staying. | *wie lange* counts as one question word.
- Ich frage mich, {{c1::ob}} das eine gute Idee war. | I wonder whether that was a good idea. | *sich fragen, ob* for internal doubt.
- Können Sie erklären, wie das {{c1::funktioniert}}? | Can you explain how that works? | *-ieren* verbs are regular and simply move to the end.
- Wir wissen noch nicht, mit wem sie {{c1::arbeitet}}. | We don't know yet whom she works with. | The preposition stays in front of the question word: *mit wem*.
- Sie hat gefragt, {{c1::wessen}} Auto vor der Tür steht. | She asked whose car is parked outside. | *wessen* is the genitive of *wer* and precedes the noun directly.
