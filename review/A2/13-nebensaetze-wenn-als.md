---
title: Nebensätze mit wenn und als
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-13-wenn-als
tags: [a2, nebensaetze, temporal]
---

- {{c1::Als}} ich klein war, wohnten wir in Rom. | When I was little, we lived in Rome. | **als** = a single event or period **in the past**.
- {{c1::Wenn}} ich Zeit habe, rufe ich dich an. | When I have time, I'll call you. | **wenn** for present, future, or repeated past events.
- Immer {{c1::wenn}} es regnet, bleibe ich drinnen. | Whenever it rains, I stay inside. | *immer wenn* stresses repetition — never *als*.
- {{c1::Als}} sie den Brief öffnete, weinte sie. | When she opened the letter, she cried. | One-off past action → *als*, with the Präteritum in writing.
- {{c1::Wenn}} das Paket ankommt, schreibe ich dir. | When the parcel arrives, I'll text you. | *wenn* also covers English *if* in real conditions.
- {{c1::Als}} der Film zu Ende war, gingen alle nach Hause. | When the film ended, everyone went home. | Front-position subordinate clause → main verb immediately after the comma.
- Ich freue mich, {{c1::wenn}} du kommst. | I'm glad when/if you come. | The subordinate clause can also follow the main clause.
- {{c1::Bevor}} ich gehe, räume ich auf. | Before I leave, I'll tidy up. | *bevor* + verb-final, like all subordinating conjunctions.
- {{c1::Nachdem}} wir gegessen hatten, gingen wir spazieren. | After we had eaten, we went for a walk. | *nachdem* usually pairs the **Plusquamperfekt** with a Präteritum main clause.
- {{c1::Während}} sie kocht, deckt er den Tisch. | While she cooks, he sets the table. | *während* for two simultaneous actions.
- {{c1::Bis}} der Bus kommt, warten wir hier. | Until the bus comes, we'll wait here. | *bis* as a conjunction sends the verb to the end.
- {{c1::Seit}} ich hier arbeite, bin ich zufriedener. | Since I've worked here, I've been happier. | *seit* as a conjunction + **present tense** for something ongoing.
- {{c1::Wenn}} ich damals mehr geübt hätte, wäre es leichter. | If I had practised more back then, it would be easier. | *wenn* also introduces unreal conditions with the Konjunktiv II.
- {{c1::Als}} Kind habe ich viel gelesen. | As a child I read a lot. | *als* + noun with **no article** means "in the role of".
