---
title: Adjektive nach bestimmtem Artikel
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-08-adj-bestimmt
tags: [a2, adjektivdeklination]
---

- Der {{c1::neue}} Kollege kommt aus Polen. | The new colleague is from Poland. | After *der* in the nominative masculine the adjective takes **-e**.
- Die {{c1::junge}} Ärztin arbeitet nachts. | The young doctor works nights. | Feminine nominative after *die* → **-e**.
- Das {{c1::kleine}} Kind schläft schon. | The little child is already asleep. | Neuter nominative after *das* → **-e**. All three genders share *-e* here.
- Ich kenne den {{c1::neuen}} Chef nicht. | I don't know the new boss. | Masculine **accusative** is the only singular slot that switches to *-en*.
- Wir sprechen über die {{c1::aktuelle}} Lage. | We're talking about the current situation. | Feminine accusative keeps **-e**, same as the nominative.
- Er sitzt auf dem {{c1::alten}} Stuhl. | He's sitting on the old chair. | Every **dative** takes *-en* after a definite article.
- Die {{c1::interessanten}} Bücher stehen oben. | The interesting books are up there. | All **plural** forms after *die* take *-en*.
- Ich habe das {{c1::ganze}} Wochenende gearbeitet. | I worked the whole weekend. | Neuter accusative → *-e*; the phrase is an accusative of duration.
- Wie war der {{c1::erste}} Eindruck? | How was the first impression? | Ordinal numbers decline like adjectives: *der erste, den ersten*.
- Sie wohnt in der {{c1::dritten}} Etage. | She lives on the third floor. | Feminine dative → *-en*, so *der dritten Etage*.
- Wir helfen den {{c1::neuen}} Studenten. | We help the new students. | Dative plural: article *den*, adjective *-en*, noun *-n*. Three markers.
- Das ist die {{c1::beste}} Lösung. | That's the best solution. | Superlatives are ordinary adjectives once an article precedes them.
- Ich nehme den {{c1::billigeren}} Flug. | I'll take the cheaper flight. | Comparative + masculine accusative ending: *billiger* + **-en**.
- Trotz des {{c1::schlechten}} Wetters gehen wir raus. | Despite the bad weather we're going out. | **Genitive** after a definite article also takes *-en*.
