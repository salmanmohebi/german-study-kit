title: Dativ
level: A2
topic_id: r-a2-01-dativ

- Ich gebe {{c1::dem}} Kind einen Apfel. | I give the child an apple. | Dative articles: *der/das → **dem***, *die → der*, plural → *den + -n*.
- Sie erzählt {{c1::der}} Kollegin die Neuigkeiten. | She tells her colleague the news. | Feminine dative is **der** — the same word as masculine nominative.
- Wir helfen {{c1::den}} Nachbarn beim Umzug. | We're helping the neighbours with the move. | Dative plural takes **den** *and* an *-n* on the noun.
- Das Buch gehört {{c1::meinem}} Vater. | The book belongs to my father. | *gehören* is a pure dative verb — no accusative object at all.
- Kannst du {{c1::mir}} das erklären? | Can you explain that to me? | Pronoun order: **dative pronoun before** the accusative *das*.
- Ich schicke {{c1::ihr}} eine Nachricht. | I'm sending her a message. | *sie* → **ihr** in the dative; *ihm* is the masculine equivalent.
- Er zeigt {{c1::uns}} seine Fotos. | He is showing us his photos. | With two noun objects the **dative comes first**: *zeigt uns seine Fotos*.
- Das Kleid passt {{c1::dir}} sehr gut. | The dress suits you very well. | *passen, stehen, gefallen* all take the dative person.
- Ich danke {{c1::Ihnen}} für die Hilfe. | I thank you for the help. | Formal dative **Ihnen**, capitalized; *danken* + dative.
- Wie geht es {{c1::deinem}} Bruder? | How is your brother? | The impersonal *es geht* + dative person is fixed.
- Wir bringen {{c1::den}} Gästen etwas zu trinken. | We're bringing the guests something to drink. | *Gästen* = plural *Gäste* + the compulsory dative *-n*.
- Sie antwortet {{c1::ihrem}} Chef nicht. | She isn't answering her boss. | *antworten* + dative, though English "answer" takes a direct object.
- Das Handy gehört {{c1::keinem}} von uns. | The phone belongs to none of us. | *kein-* in the masculine dative → **keinem**.
- Ich glaube {{c1::dem}} Bericht nicht. | I don't believe the report. | *glauben* + dative for a person or source you trust.
