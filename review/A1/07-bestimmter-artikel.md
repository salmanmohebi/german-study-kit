---
title: Bestimmter Artikel + Genus
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-07-bestimmter-artikel
tags: [a1, artikel, genus]
---

- {{c1::Die}} Bibliothek ist heute geschlossen. | The library is closed today. | Nouns ending in **-thek, -ung, -heit, -keit, -ion** are feminine.
- {{c1::Der}} Frühling beginnt im März. | Spring begins in March. | Seasons, months and days of the week are all **masculine**.
- {{c1::Das}} Mädchen wartet auf den Bus. | The girl is waiting for the bus. | Diminutives in **-chen/-lein** are neuter, regardless of real gender.
- {{c1::Die}} Freiheit ist wichtig. | Freedom is important. | The suffix **-heit** makes abstract feminine nouns.
- {{c1::Das}} Wasser ist sehr kalt. | The water is very cold. | Most nouns ending in **-er** that name substances are neuter; check the article when learning.
- {{c1::Der}} Computer steht auf dem Tisch. | The computer is on the table. | Agent nouns and many *-er* nouns are masculine: *der Lehrer, der Drucker*.
- {{c1::Die}} Zeitung liegt auf dem Sofa. | The newspaper is on the sofa. | **-ung** is always feminine — a reliable rule with no exceptions.
- {{c1::Das}} Studium dauert vier Jahre. | The degree course lasts four years. | Latin loans in **-um** are neuter: *das Museum, das Zentrum*.
- {{c1::Die}} Universität liegt im Zentrum. | The university is in the centre. | **-tät** (from Latin *-tas*) is feminine, like *die Qualität*.
- {{c1::Der}} Käse schmeckt gut. | The cheese tastes good. | Learn each noun **with** its article — gender is not predictable from meaning.
- {{c1::Das}} Restaurant öffnet um elf. | The restaurant opens at eleven. | French loans in **-ant/-ment** are usually neuter.
- {{c1::Die}} Kinder spielen draußen. | The children are playing outside. | In the plural **all** genders take *die*.
- {{c1::Der}} Kühlschrank ist leer. | The fridge is empty. | In a compound noun the **last** part sets the gender: *der Schrank* → *der Kühlschrank*.
- {{c1::Das}} Handy liegt in der Tasche. | The mobile phone is in the bag. | Nouns ending in *-y* borrowed into German are mostly neuter.
- {{c1::Die}} Möglichkeit besteht. | The possibility exists. | **-keit** is feminine, like its cousin *-heit*.
- {{c1::Der}} Winter war sehr mild. | The winter was very mild. | Weather words like *der Regen, der Schnee, der Wind* are masculine.
