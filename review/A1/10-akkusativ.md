---
title: Akkusativ
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-10-akkusativ

---

- Ich lese {{c1::den}} Artikel später. | I'll read the article later. | Masculine *der* → **den** in the accusative; other genders are unchanged.
- Sie besucht {{c1::ihren}} Onkel. | She is visiting her uncle. | Possessives follow *ein-*: *ihr* → **ihren** before a masculine accusative noun.
- Wir nehmen {{c1::die}} Straßenbahn. | We're taking the tram. | Feminine *die* stays **die** in the accusative — no change to memorize.
- Er trägt {{c1::das}} schwere Paket. | He is carrying the heavy parcel. | Neuter *das* is identical in nominative and accusative.
- Kennst du {{c1::diesen}} Film? | Do you know this film? | *dieser* declines like *der*: masculine accusative → **diesen**.
- Ich frage {{c1::den}} Kellner. | I'll ask the waiter. | *fragen* takes the **accusative**, unlike *antworten*, which takes the dative.
- Sie liebt {{c1::ihn}} sehr. | She loves him very much. | The accusative pronoun for *er* is **ihn**.
- Ruf {{c1::mich}} bitte morgen an. | Please call me tomorrow. | *ich* → **mich** in the accusative; *mir* would be dative.
- Der Hund beißt {{c1::keinen}} Menschen. | The dog doesn't bite anybody. | *der Mensch* is an n-noun: accusative **Menschen** plus *keinen*.
- Wir suchen {{c1::unseren}} Hausschlüssel. | We're looking for our house key. | *unser* → **unseren**; the ending goes on the possessive, not the noun.
- Ich verstehe {{c1::dich}} am Telefon schlecht. | I can hardly understand you on the phone. | *du* → **dich** as a direct object.
- Sie hat {{c1::uns}} eingeladen. | She invited us. | **uns** serves as both accusative and dative — one form to learn.
- Ich brauche {{c1::einen}} Termin beim Arzt. | I need an appointment with the doctor. | *brauchen* + accusative; *beim* = *bei dem* (dative).
- Er stellt {{c1::die}} Frage noch einmal. | He asks the question once again. | Feminine accusative *die*; *noch einmal* sits after the object.
- Ich mag {{c1::euch}} alle. | I like you all. | The *ihr* accusative form is **euch**, also used as the dative.
- Trink {{c1::das}} Wasser aus. | Drink up the water. | *austrinken* is separable; the neuter object *das Wasser* is unchanged.
