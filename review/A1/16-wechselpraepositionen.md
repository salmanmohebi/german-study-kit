---
title: Wechselpräpositionen (Wo/Wohin)
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-16-wechselpraepositionen
tags: [a1, praepositionen, akkusativ, dativ]
---

- Die Uhr hängt über {{c1::dem}} Sofa. | The clock hangs above the sofa. | Position (*wo?*) → **dative**: *über dem Tisch*.
- Ich hänge das Bild über {{c1::den}} Schreibtisch. | I'm hanging the picture above the desk. | Direction (*wohin?*) → **accusative**: *über den Schreibtisch*.
- Die Katze schläft unter {{c1::dem}} Bett. | The cat is sleeping under the bed. | *schlafen* is static → dative.
- Der Ball rollt unter {{c1::das}} Sofa. | The ball rolls under the sofa. | Movement to a new place → accusative, even for neuter *das*.
- Wir sitzen in {{c1::der}} Küche. | We are sitting in the kitchen. | *sitzen* = position → dative feminine **der**.
- Ich gehe in {{c1::die}} Küche. | I'm going into the kitchen. | *gehen in* + accusative → *die Küche*.
- Das Auto steht vor {{c1::dem}} Haus. | The car is parked in front of the house. | *stehen* is static → dative; *vor dem* often contracts in speech only.
- Stell die Schuhe vor {{c1::die}} Tür. | Put the shoes in front of the door. | *stellen* is a directional verb → accusative.
- Der Schlüssel liegt auf {{c1::dem}} Regal. | The key is lying on the shelf. | *liegen* (static) → dative; *legen* (directional) → accusative.
- Leg den Schlüssel auf {{c1::den}} Tisch. | Put the key on the table. | *legen* + accusative — the classic *liegen/legen* pair.
- Sie wartet an {{c1::der}} Haltestelle. | She is waiting at the stop. | *warten an* = position → dative.
- Ich fahre an {{c1::den}} See. | I'm driving to the lake. | Destination → accusative *an den See*; back home it's *am See* (dative).
- Zwischen {{c1::den}} Häusern liegt ein Garten. | Between the houses there is a garden. | Dative **plural** adds *-n* to the noun: *den Häusern*.
- Er stellt die Flasche zwischen {{c1::die}} Gläser. | He puts the bottle between the glasses. | Directional plural accusative → *die Gläser*, no extra *-n*.
- Das Kind läuft hinter {{c1::das}} Haus. | The child runs behind the house. | *laufen* with a goal → accusative.
- Der Garten liegt hinter {{c1::dem}} Haus. | The garden is behind the house. | *liegen* describes location → dative. Ask *wo?* to choose the case.
