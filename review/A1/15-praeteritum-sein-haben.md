---
title: Präteritum von sein, haben, Modalverben
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-15-praeteritum
tags: [a1, praeteritum, modalverben]
---

- Ich {{c1::war}} gestern zu Hause. | I was at home yesterday. | *sein* and *haben* prefer the **Präteritum** even in speech: *war*, not *bin gewesen*.
- {{c1::Waren}} Sie schon einmal in Italien? | Have you been to Italy before? | Präteritum of *sein*: *war, warst, war, waren, wart, waren*.
- Wir {{c1::hatten}} keine andere Wahl. | We had no other choice. | *haben → hatte*; the stem loses its *b* and takes **-t-**.
- Er {{c1::konnte}} nicht schlafen. | He couldn't sleep. | Modals in the Präteritum **drop the umlaut** and add *-te*: *können → konnte*.
- Sie {{c1::musste}} früher gehen. | She had to leave earlier. | *müssen → musste*. This is far more common than *hat müssen*.
- Ich {{c1::wollte}} dich nicht stören. | I didn't want to disturb you. | *wollen → wollte*, a polite everyday softener.
- Es {{c1::war}} sehr windig. | It was very windy. | Impersonal *es war* for past weather.
- {{c1::Hattest}} du damals ein Auto? | Did you have a car back then? | *du hattest*; the *-te-* marker sits before the personal ending.
- Wir {{c1::durften}} nicht fotografieren. | We weren't allowed to take photos. | *dürfen → durfte*, umlaut gone.
- Ihr {{c1::wart}} sehr hilfsbereit. | You all were very helpful. | *ihr wart* — no *-e-*, unlike *ihr hattet*.
- Ich {{c1::hatte}} großen Hunger. | I was very hungry. | *Hunger haben* keeps *haben* in every tense.
- Sie {{c1::sollten}} eigentlich um acht kommen. | They were actually supposed to come at eight. | *sollen → sollte*, often used for a plan that failed.
- Das {{c1::war}} eine gute Entscheidung. | That was a good decision. | *war* + a nominative predicate after *sein*.
- Wir {{c1::hatten}} damals kein Internet. | We had no internet back then. | *damals* signals a past period and pairs naturally with the Präteritum.
- Er {{c1::mochte}} das Essen nicht. | He didn't like the food. | *mögen → mochte*, with **ch** in the past stem.
- Ich {{c1::wusste}} das nicht. | I didn't know that. | *wissen → wusste*, patterning with the modals.
