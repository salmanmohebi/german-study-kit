---
title: Pluralformen
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-09-plural
tags: [a1, plural, nomen]
---

- Die {{c1::Tische}} sind aus Holz. | The tables are made of wood. | Many masculine nouns take **-e**: *der Tisch → die Tische*.
- Zwei {{c1::Bücher}} liegen auf dem Boden. | Two books are lying on the floor. | Neuter nouns often take **-er + umlaut**: *das Buch → die Bücher*.
- Die {{c1::Frauen}} arbeiten im Labor. | The women work in the lab. | Feminine nouns usually take **-(e)n**: *die Frau → die Frauen*.
- Alle {{c1::Zimmer}} haben ein Bad. | All the rooms have a bathroom. | Nouns in *-er, -en, -el* often take **no ending** in the plural.
- Die {{c1::Autos}} stehen vor dem Haus. | The cars are parked in front of the house. | Loanwords and words in *-o* take **-s**: *das Auto → die Autos*.
- Wir haben drei {{c1::Kinder}}. | We have three children. | *das Kind → die Kinder*, **no** umlaut, unlike *Bücher*.
- Die {{c1::Städte}} im Süden sind wärmer. | The cities in the south are warmer. | *die Stadt → die Städte* — a feminine noun with **umlaut + -e**, unusual.
- Meine {{c1::Freundinnen}} kommen später. | My female friends are coming later. | Nouns in **-in** double the *n*: *die Freundin → die Freundinnen*.
- Die {{c1::Äpfel}} sind reif. | The apples are ripe. | Some masculine nouns take **only an umlaut**: *der Apfel → die Äpfel*.
- Beide {{c1::Häuser}} wurden verkauft. | Both houses were sold. | *das Haus → die Häuser*, the classic neuter *-er* + umlaut pattern.
- Die {{c1::Länder}} unterschreiben den Vertrag. | The countries are signing the treaty. | *das Land → die Länder*; in the dative plural you would add *-n*: *den Ländern*.
- Ich mag deine {{c1::Ideen}}. | I like your ideas. | *die Idee* already ends in *-e*, so the plural adds only **-n**.
- Die {{c1::Männer}} warten draußen. | The men are waiting outside. | *der Mann → die Männer* — masculine with *-er* + umlaut, a small group.
- Zwei {{c1::Handys}} sind kaputt. | Two mobile phones are broken. | English loans keep the **-s** plural in German too.
- Die {{c1::Ärzte}} machen eine Pause. | The doctors are taking a break. | *der Arzt → die Ärzte*, umlaut + *-e*.
- Alle {{c1::Studenten}} bekommen eine Mail. | All the students get an email. | *der Student* is an **n-declension** noun: *-en* in the plural and in all singular cases but nominative.
