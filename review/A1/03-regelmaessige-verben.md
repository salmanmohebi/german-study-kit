title: Regelmäßige Verben (Präsens)
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-03-regelmaessig
tags: [a1, verb-praesens, konjugation]
---

- Ich {{c1::arbeite}} in einem Büro. | I work in an office. | Stems in *-t* or *-d* insert an **-e-**: *arbeit-e-st, arbeit-e-t*.
- Du {{c1::wohnst}} in der Nähe. | You live nearby. | Regular endings: *-e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en*.
- Er {{c1::spielt}} jeden Abend Gitarre. | He plays guitar every evening. | Time expression (*jeden Abend*) before object — **Te-Ka-Mo-Lo** order.
- Wir {{c1::lernen}} zusammen Deutsch. | We learn German together. | *wir* and *sie/Sie* always take the **infinitive form** *-en*.
- Ihr {{c1::wartet}} schon lange. | You all have been waiting a long time. | *warten* ends in *-t*, so *ihr* gets **-et**: *wartet*.
- Sie {{c1::kaufen}} ein neues Auto. | They are buying a new car. | German has one present tense — it covers both *buy* and *are buying*.
- Ich {{c1::öffne}} das Fenster. | I open the window. | Stems ending in *-n* after a consonant also insert **-e-**: *öffn-e-st*.
- Du {{c1::reist}} viel. | You travel a lot. | A stem ending in *-s* takes only **-t** for *du*: *reist*, not *reisst*.
- Meine Mutter {{c1::kocht}} sehr gut. | My mother cooks very well. | Singular noun subject = *er/sie/es* form.
- Wir {{c1::feiern}} am Samstag. | We are celebrating on Saturday. | Verbs in *-ern/-eln* keep **-n** for *wir/sie*: *feiern*, not *feieren*.
- {{c1::Verstehst}} du die Frage? | Do you understand the question? | German needs **no** *do*-auxiliary for questions.
- Er {{c1::bezahlt}} mit Karte. | He pays by card. | Inseparable prefix *be-* is never split and takes no *ge-* in the Perfekt.
- Ich {{c1::sammle}} alte Münzen. | I collect old coins. | *sammeln* drops the *e* for *ich*: **sammle**.
- Ihr {{c1::antwortet}} zu schnell. | You all answer too quickly. | *antworten*: *-t* stem → *antwort-e-t*.
- Sie {{c1::heißt}} Anna. | Her name is Anna. | *heißen* has *ß*, so *du* and *er/sie/es* share the form **heißt**.
- Wir {{c1::brauchen}} mehr Papier. | We need more paper. | **mehr** is invariable — it never takes an ending.
