---
title: Trennbare Verben
level: A1
topic_id: r-a1-06-trennbar
tags: [a1, trennbare-verben, wortstellung]
---

- Der Film {{c1::fängt}} um acht Uhr an. | The film starts at eight o'clock. | *anfangen*: the prefix **an** goes to the very end of the main clause.
- Ich {{c1::stehe}} morgens um sechs auf. | I get up at six in the morning. | *aufstehen* splits; the time expression sits between verb and prefix.
- {{c1::Rufst}} du mich später an? | Will you call me later? | In a question the conjugated part leads, the prefix still ends the clause.
- Wir {{c1::räumen}} das Wohnzimmer auf. | We are tidying up the living room. | *aufräumen* takes an accusative object placed before the prefix.
- Sie {{c1::zieht}} nächsten Monat um. | She is moving house next month. | *umziehen* — one word in the infinitive, split in a conjugated clause.
- Der Bus {{c1::kommt}} in zehn Minuten an. | The bus arrives in ten minutes. | *ankommen* ≠ *kommen*: the prefix changes the meaning to *arrive*.
- Bitte {{c1::machen}} Sie das Licht aus. | Please switch off the light. | Formal imperative: **verb + Sie**, prefix at the end.
- Ich {{c1::kaufe}} noch Brot ein. | I'll buy some bread. | *einkaufen* means shopping in general; *kaufen* alone means to buy.
- Wann {{c1::fährt}} der Zug ab? | When does the train depart? | *abfahren* = to depart; note the vowel change *a → ä* as well.
- Er {{c1::zieht}} seine Jacke an. | He puts on his jacket. | *anziehen* for putting clothes on; *ausziehen* to take them off.
- Ich muss den Termin {{c1::absagen}}. | I have to cancel the appointment. | After a modal the separable verb stays **whole** in the infinitive.
- {{c1::Steigen}} Sie am Hauptbahnhof um. | Change trains at the main station. | *umsteigen* = to change trains; *einsteigen* / *aussteigen* to board / get off.
- Sie {{c1::hört}} nie mit dem Reden auf. | She never stops talking. | *aufhören mit* + **dative**; *nie* goes before the object phrase.
- Wir {{c1::laden}} unsere Nachbarn ein. | We are inviting our neighbours. | *einladen* → *er lädt ein* with *a → ä* in the singular.
- Das Konzert {{c1::findet}} im Park statt. | The concert takes place in the park. | *stattfinden* is used only with things, never with people as subject.
- Ich {{c1::schlage}} einen Kompromiss vor. | I suggest a compromise. | *vorschlagen* → *ich schlage ... vor*, with the object in the middle field.
