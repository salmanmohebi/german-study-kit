- {{c1::Komm}} bitte herein! | Please come in! | The *du*-imperative is the stem **without** *-st* and without the pronoun.
- {{c1::Nehmen}} Sie bitte Platz. | Please take a seat. | The formal imperative keeps **Sie** after the verb: *Nehmen Sie*.
- {{c1::Macht}} eure Hausaufgaben! | Do your homework! | The *ihr*-imperative is identical to the present tense, minus *ihr*.
- {{c1::Sprich}} bitte langsamer. | Please speak more slowly. | Verbs with *e → i* keep the change in the imperative: *sprechen → sprich*.
- {{c1::Fahr}} nicht so schnell! | Don't drive so fast! | Verbs with *a → ä* **lose** the umlaut in the imperative: *fahr*, not *fähr*.
- {{c1::Sei}} bitte ehrlich. | Please be honest. | *sein* has irregular imperatives: **sei / seien Sie / seid**.
- {{c1::Warte}} einen Moment. | Wait a moment. | Stems in *-t* keep the **-e**: *warte*, *arbeite*, *öffne*.
- {{c1::Gib}} mir bitte das Salz. | Please pass me the salt. | *geben → gib*; dative *mir* precedes the accusative noun.
- {{c1::Steh}} bitte früher auf. | Please get up earlier. | Separable prefix still goes to the **end**: *Steh ... auf*.
- {{c1::Haben}} Sie keine Angst. | Don't be afraid. | Negative commands use *kein-* with a noun, *nicht* with a verb.
- {{c1::Lies}} den Vertrag genau. | Read the contract carefully. | *lesen → lies*, no *-e* despite the *-s* stem.
- {{c1::Vergiss}} nicht, das Licht auszumachen. | Don't forget to switch off the light. | *zu + infinitive* of a separable verb becomes **auszumachen**, one word.
- {{c1::Setzen}} wir uns nach vorne. | Let's sit at the front. | The *wir*-imperative (verb + *wir*) is the German "let's".
- {{c1::Hör}} mir bitte zu. | Please listen to me. | *zuhören* takes the **dative**: *mir zuhören*, not *mich*.
- {{c1::Bringt}} bitte etwas zu trinken mit. | Please bring something to drink. | *mitbringen* separates; *etwas zu trinken* is a fixed infinitive phrase.
- {{c1::Entschuldigen}} Sie die Störung. | Excuse the interruption. | Formal polite formula — very common at the start of a request.
